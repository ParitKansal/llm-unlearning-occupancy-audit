# Idea description given to every novelty check (2026-10-05)

Occupancy models with imperfect detection (ecology; MacKenzie et al. 2002, Royle-Nichols 2003) x LLM unlearning / knowledge probing.
    Idea: a single probe failing to elicit a fact is "non-detection", not "absence". Repeated diverse probes (paraphrases, languages, few-shot formats, light relearning) are repeated site visits; an occupancy model separates psi (fact still stored) from p (probe detects it) and estimates residual-knowledge prevalence after unlearning, with CIs. Validated against relearning-attack ground truth.
    Synonyms to search: occupancy model, imperfect detection, detection probability, non-detection, site occupancy, zero-inflated, latent presence, "knowledge is hidden not removed", residual knowledge estimation, unlearning evaluation with repeated probes, latent knowledge prevalence.
